Experience

Over the years I've done my fair share of research and learning in the world of IT and tech. Mostly I've been teaching myself how to build computers, use Linux, flash android phones, softmod and hardmod consoles and handheld devices and so much more. The first time I fixed a PC was when I was just 5 years old. We were at a friend of my mom's house and they had a computer that did not function on the room of her daughter. I entered the bios, under-clocked the CPU and RAM and the PC started to function again. My parents and her parents were stunned that I managed to fix it since her father had been trying for days to get it to work again.

I've also done a lot of homelabbing to automate certain things and make my life a little easier.

I will never stop and always continue learning in this field.
