## Full control of hardware I bought

I like console games, and I like having control of the device they run on. Mods, save files, homebrew, and backups of games I own.

This was the first console I modified. My dad was not happy about it at first. He changed his mind. The whole thing is an SD card and a few files.

## The first step

The files come from [LetterBomb](https://please.hackmii.com/). The site asks for the system version and the MAC address, then it hands over a zip.

> The files go on the SD card, the card goes into the Wii, and the message board gets scrolled back two days. A red letter with a bomb in it should be sitting there.

## Installing it

Opening the letter continues the install. I said yes to the Homebrew Channel, then installed BootMii as IOS, confirmed twice, and also installed BootMii as boot2. Return to the menu, then exit.

The Wii boots into the Homebrew Channel.

## Final steps

I do not want a brick. The NAND, which holds the operating system, gets a backup before I experiment.

The clear version of that procedure is on [Wii Guide](https://wii.hacks.guide/nand-backup).
